About Me

Building my foundation in technology.

I am Shruti Kumari, a Computer Science and Engineering student interested in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Analytics and Cybersecurity.

I am currently pursuing my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering at Amity University, with an expected graduation year of 2027.

My academic journey has given me exposure to programming, databases, data analysis, artificial intelligence, machine learning and web technologies. I primarily work with Python and have also developed a foundation in SQL, HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

I enjoy learning through practical projects. My project experience includes AI-powered applications, computer vision, data-driven systems and web applications.

One of my major projects is the Consumer Attention Mapping System, developed during the Infosys Springboard internship. The project focuses on analysing shopper behaviour through computer vision and AI-based techniques.

I am also exploring cybersecurity and strengthening my understanding of areas that connect technology, analysis and real-world problem solving.

Beyond technical skills, I value analytical thinking, problem solving, adaptability, teamwork and continuous learning.
